# CLUE block vintages and annual employment

Reviewed 2026-09-29. The City of Melbourne [employment dataset](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/employment-by-block-by-clue-industry/) covers census years 2002–2024. Its `census_year` says when surveying was completed; it does not identify a precise observation day. Blank industry cells are suppressed and must remain null. The separate [CLUE blocks layer](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/blocks-for-census-of-land-use-and-employment-clue/) says its boundaries were realigned and modified during 2025.

The connector therefore keeps the employment year as a period and does not invent a December 31 observation timestamp. It stores `block_id` and `clue_small_area`, but marks the spatial key `CLUE_BLOCK_UNRESOLVED:<block_id>:<year>` with no `source_area_vintage`. The current boundary layer is labelled as the 2025 alignment. A request for a historic polygon vintage fails rather than returning that current geometry under an old year.

Do not map 2002–2024 jobs onto the 2025 polygons solely by equal block ID. A historical boundary release or an official, year-specific crosswalk is needed before those indicators can be located at block geometry. Until then the BigQuery area view should report `boundary_mapping_status = missing`; source year and job values remain queryable. The employment connector remains `review_required`. The separate blocks connector has [its own licence and live publication review](clue-blocks-admission.md) and is `verified_open` / `active` in the development dataset.
