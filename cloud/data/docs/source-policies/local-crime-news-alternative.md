# Local crime news alternative investigation

Alternative publisher review: 2026-09-30. GDELT and Victoria Police evidence refreshed 2026-10-01. **No replacement source is selected for T051.** Six distinct Melbourne crime reports were found in actual complete GKG windows and their original publication instants were independently checked against bounded publisher-head metadata. The release integrator confirmed actual BigQuery readback of six reviewed reports on six dates, plus 1,620 CSA regional indicators. This removes the earlier unknown discovery numerator; it does not establish comprehensive incident coverage or the independent SC-004 benchmark. ## 2026-10-01 release decision

The six fixed crime reports provide a real sample for the recent-30-day minimum, with six distinct original publication dates and explicit Melbourne reporting context. They include investigations, sentencing, a trial, charges, and a charge withdrawal. These report types must remain visible so a recently published court story is not presented as a new incident or a withdrawn charge as a conviction.

There is no reason to add a restricted alternative solely to fill the sample numerator. T051 remains conditional. Victoria Police was re-read on 2026-10-01; its current disclaimer and State Government copyright footer do not provide a source-specific licence for an automated news feed. No Police connector, journalist subscription, paywall bypass, or publisher body scraper was added.

For ongoing GKG updates, a conservative metadata classifier has been implemented and tested using the [official theme lookup](https://data.gdeltproject.org/api/v2/guides/LOOKUP-GKGTHEMES.TXT): official City/Greater Melbourne mention plus an exact crime-theme match plus an explicit crime headline term. New rows carry `gdelt_theme_candidate`, unverified occurrence and review-required markers. They do not gain invented publication times, event points or verified-sample status. This uses existing GDELT dataset fields and adds no new publisher feed or permission claim. The release integrator deploys the classifier update separately from the already published six-report sample.
